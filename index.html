Tallinn moves

Find your bus on the map, check the next departure and follow your route. Strada brings the city’s transport to your Android phone.

One map. Your everyday routes.

01 / Live map

Less guessing
More going

See where vehicles are on the map. Switch between buses, trams and trains, then tap a vehicle to find out where it’s heading.

Vehicle positions come from transport data feeds. Availability depends on the operator.

02 / Stop departures

Your stop
The next departures

Tap a stop to see what’s coming. Routes, destinations and departure times sit together, so you can check your connection before walking out the door.

From Tõnismägi to your daily stop, the next ride is a tap away.

03 / Route view

Know what’s
around the next stop

Open a vehicle’s route to see its stops and upcoming arrival information. Save the routes you use most and find them again when it’s time to head home.

Your familiar journey, with the details close at hand.

04 / Journey planning

From your first step
to your last stop

Choose your destination and compare public transport journeys and walking routes. See the transfers, departure times and stops along the way.

Made for Tallinn

An independent app
A shared city

Strada is a personal project by StasMega, built for everyday public transport journeys in Tallinn. It’s open source, made for Android and free of ads.

Transport information comes from public data sources, including Tallinn’s transport feeds and Elron. Live information can be delayed; always check operator notices for service changes.
